🚢 Ocean Freight CORE SERVICE

Our primary business — reliable global sea freight from China and Vietnam to the United States and Canada. We offer Full Container Load (FCL) and Less than Container Load (LCL) with weekly sailings, competitive all-in rates, and door-to-door DDP options.

  • 🇨🇳 China → USA: Shanghai, Ningbo, Yantian → LA/NY/SAV/MEM/ORD/Chicago/Memphis
  • 🇻🇳 Vietnam → USA: Ho Chi Minh City (HCM) → LA/NY/SAV/MEM/ORD/Chicago/Memphis
  • 🇨🇳 China → Canada: Shanghai/Ningbo/Yantian → Vancouver/Toronto/Calgary/Montreal
  • 🇻🇳 Vietnam → Canada: HCM → Vancouver/Toronto/Montreal
  • FCL: 20GP / 40GP / 40HC / 40HQ containers
  • LCL: From 2 CBM, weekly consolidation
  • Global transit: China→US 19–30d | China→Europe 28–40d | China→UK 30–42d | China→Australia 18–28d | China→Canada 22–28d
  • DDP service — customs & duties included
  • ISF filing, ABI customs entry, Amazon FBA delivery
  • IOR Advantage — Own customs broker license for faster clearance
