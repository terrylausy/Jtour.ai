FCL vs. LCL — Which is Right for You?

Not sure which service fits your shipment? Here's a quick guide to help you decide.

🚢 Full Container Load (FCL)

Best for: Volume > 15 CBM / High-value goods / Sensitive cargo

  • ✓ Your own container — no sharing with other cargo
  • ✓ Lower risk of damage during transit
  • ✓ Faster transit time vs. LCL (no consolidation delays)
  • ✓ Suitable for oversized or overweight cargo
  • ✓ Cost-efficient when cargo fills 70%+ of container
  • ✓ Better suited for DDP / Amazon FBA bulk orders
  • ✓ Predictable all-in pricing

📦 Less than Container Load (LCL)

Best for: Volume < 15 CBM / Frequent small shipments / Startups

  • ✓ Pay only for space you use (by CBM)
  • ✓ No need to wait until you have a full container
  • ✓ Ideal for e-commerce, Amazon FBA replenishment
  • ✓ Lower upfront shipping cost vs. FCL
  • ✓ Weekly departures — keeps inventory flowing
  • ✓ Great for sample orders or new product testing
  • ✓ Combines with air freight for split shipment strategies

📦 LCL → FCL Bundle Service

Combine multiple LCL shipments into a full container — best value for shipments between 15–60 CBM

What is LCL to FCL?

When your cargo volume is too large for LCL (over 2 CBM minimum) but doesn't fill a full container, our LCL to FCL bundle service lets you combine your shipment with other carefully vetted cargo into a shared 20GP or 40HQ container — giving you FCL-like benefits at near-LCL pricing.
