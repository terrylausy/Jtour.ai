About JTOUR

Your Trusted Global Logistics Partner

Company Profile

JTOUR Logistics is a leading global freight forwarding and logistics service provider headquartered in Cerritos, California, USA, with operations across China (Shenzhen & Dongguan) and a worldwide partner network. Since 2014, we have grown from a China-US carrier into a full-service global logistics company serving routes to North America, Europe, the UK, Australia, and beyond.

We provide comprehensive international logistics solutions — including ocean freight, air freight, door-to-door delivery, and e-commerce FBA shipping — for businesses of all sizes: from cross-border e-commerce sellers and trading companies to large-scale manufacturers. Our mission is simple: make global shipping seamless, transparent, and reliable.

Since our establishment, JTOUR has been adhering to the philosophy of "Customer First, Service Supreme". With our professional team, extensive industry experience spanning 13+ years, and a wide logistics network covering major trade lanes worldwide, we help customers save time and costs while creating greater value in every shipment.
