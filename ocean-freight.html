FCL vs. LCL — Which is Right for You?

🚢 Full Container Load (FCL) — Volume ≥ 15 CBM
  • Dedicated container — no cargo mixing
  • 20GP / 40GP / 40HC options
  • Faster transit — no consolidation delay
  • Port-to-port or door-to-door DDP
  • Factory pickup from Guangdong, Zhejiang, Jiangsu
  • Real-time container tracking
📦 LCL — Volume ≥ 2 CBM
  • Pay only for space used (by CBM)
  • Weekly consolidation from all major ports
  • Lower upfront cost vs. FCL
  • Ideal for e-commerce & retail imports
  • Professional packing & labeling available
  • Cargo insurance available
